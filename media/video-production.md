## Review gate

- [ ] Asset/likeness rights and data handling are approved.
- [ ] On-screen claims are sourced and legible.
- [ ] Captions, transcript, and audio description requirements are met.
- [ ] The result matches visual identity and contains no prohibited imagery.
- [ ] A human reviewer has approved publication where required.

## Agent rules and completion check

- May prepare scene briefs and flag rights, claims, continuity, and accessibility gaps.
- Must not use a person’s likeness, clone a voice, imitate protected material, or mark a video approved without recorded authority.
- [ ] Every scene has a purpose, approved assets, text/script source, and accessibility treatment.
- [ ] The final review records platform, version, locale, reviewer, and expiry.
