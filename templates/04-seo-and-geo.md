## Page rules

- Answer the primary question early and directly.
- Use descriptive headings, concise definitions, and internally consistent terminology.
- Cite original evidence near material claims; include author/date where relevant.
- Add structured data only when it accurately represents visible page content.
- Do not create pages solely to target keywords or AI summaries.

## GEO quality check

- [ ] A reader can identify the answer, author/source, and date.
- [ ] Claims are specific enough to quote without losing context.
- [ ] Original experience, data, or expert perspective is present where possible.
- [ ] The content is current, internally linked, and technically indexable.
