# Positioning and messaging pillars

## Purpose and when to load

Use this file to decide what the brand should consistently mean to a specific audience. Load for strategy, content, campaigns, sales material, and reviews. Positioning is a decision about the customer's alternatives; a tagline alone is not positioning.

## Positioning

We help **[ideal customer]** do **[desired outcome]** without **[common frustration or alternative]**.

- Category: [buyer-language category]

### How to complete positioning

Name the audience, category, outcome, and alternative in buyer language. Explain why the category is useful before claiming uniqueness. If a positioning claim cannot be evidenced, label it an aspiration or remove it.

## Claim discipline

- A differentiator is an observable difference; a benefit is the resulting customer value; proof is the source that supports either statement.
- Use quantified, comparative, availability, security, health, financial, or sustainability claims only with current evidence and approval rules from governance/compliance.md.
- Do not imply that all customers receive an outcome supported only by a case study or limited cohort.

## Agent rules and completion check

- May combine approved pillars for the stated audience and channel; must preserve recorded claim boundaries.
- Must not create a new pillar, competitor comparison, guarantee, or proof point without review.
