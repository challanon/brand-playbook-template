# Verbal identity

Northstar is precise, calm, and useful; never clinical, timid, or hype-driven.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Activation fell after step three. Start there." | "Unlock game-changing activation insights." |
| Name uncertainty and its next check. | Present an estimate as certainty. |

Support is reassuring and specific. Sales pages are confident but evidence-led. Incident updates are factual, timely, and accountable.

**Approved:** “Three teams reduced setup drop-off after making the first report a guided task.” Why: a concrete outcome with defined scope.

**Avoid:** “The only analytics platform you’ll ever need.” Why: an unsupported absolute claim.
