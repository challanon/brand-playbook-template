# Positioning and messaging pillars

We help product leaders at 50–500-person B2B SaaS companies turn behavioural data into the next product decision without a reporting backlog.

| Pillar | Message | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision-ready | Start with the decision, not the dashboard. | Saved report templates link metrics to a decision owner. |
| Trustworthy | Definitions travel with every metric. | Every shared metric exposes its event and calculation. |
| Adoptable | Teams can answer routine questions without an analyst queue. | Guided questions and role-specific views. |

Do not name competitors outside explicit comparison pages approved by product marketing.
