# Conversion journeys

| Stage | User need | CTA | Friction to remove | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Understand activation measurement | Read the guide | Explain terms before asking for a demo | Qualified guide completion |
| Compare | Judge fit and effort | See a tailored demo | Show setup steps and data requirements | Demo-to-qualified-opportunity rate |

## Demo-form journey contract

The form asks only for work email, company size, and primary use case. It explains why each field is requested, supports keyboard completion, gives inline errors in text, and confirms the next step and expected response time after submission.
