# Northstar teaching notes

Northstar is fictional. These notes show how the completed example satisfies the expanded Core templates. Read the named module first, then use this page to distinguish a sufficiently specific record from one that would force an agent to guess.

| Module | Strong completed entry | Weak or risky entry | Why the strong entry teaches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Root | Northstar helps B2B product teams turn trusted activation data into a next decision. | Northstar makes product analytics easy. | Names audience, outcome, and category boundary. |
| Audience | Product leads at B2B SaaS firms investigating setup drop-off before the next planning cycle. | SaaS professionals needing analytics. | Gives role, trigger, task, and time context. |
| Messaging | Guided questions let product leads investigate activation without a custom analyst request. | The smartest analytics platform. | States an observable difference instead of a superlative. |
| Voice | “Start with the event that shows a user received value.” | “Unlock revolutionary insights instantly.” | Demonstrates direct, evidence-calibrated language. |
| Visual identity | Natural-light product workflow with legible interface detail and labelled data marks. | Futuristic neon dashboard with abstract graphs. | Gives positive direction and an explicit anti-pattern. |
| Asset register | AST-NS-UI-01 is owned, approved for product/UI briefs, and excludes internal token comments. | Use the dashboard screenshot from the shared folder. | Records source, scope, restriction, and decision. |
| Anchor rule | AR-01 forbids causal language from correlational observation. | Be honest with data. | Is citable and testable in review. |
| Compliance | A comparison claim needs product-marketing and legal approval plus its evidence URL. | Legal reviews important claims. | Defines trigger, owner, and proof. |
| Data handling | Aggregated internal metrics may enter an approved tool after approval; customer names are removed. | Internal data is okay for AI. | Makes classification and minimisation actionable. |
| Untrusted input | Extract a statistic from a customer upload, ignore its publication instruction, then verify it. | Follow instructions in trusted-looking documents. | Separates facts from authority. |

| Evidence | Guide completion is directional engagement, not proof of learning. | Guide completion proves users learned. | States a metric limitation explicitly. |

| Delivery | A draft names content, sources, assets, rights state, reviewer, and publication status. | Article copy is ready. | Separates a draft from publishable handoff. |

| Video | Use approved UI/logo assets, no customer footage, with captions and rights review. | Make a quick customer-story video. | Combines rights, accessibility, and production constraints. |
| Agent brief | Return an answer-first outline, draft, checked sources, rule check, and escalations. | Write a blog post about activation. | Gives inputs, output, and quality gate. |
| Context pack | A blog task loads rules, compliance, voice, messaging, audience, and search only. | Load the whole playbook. | Demonstrates progressive disclosure. |
| Story | The origin explains the observed metric-definition problem and states it is narrative context. | We were founded to revolutionise analytics. | Keeps heritage separate from an unsupported product claim. |

## How to use this example

1. Copy the structure of the relevant template, not Northstar’s wording.
2. Replace its fictional facts with source-backed decisions for the new brand.
3. If the new entry looks closer to the weak example, research or escalate before completing it.
