# Sources and metrics

| Item | Source of truth | Owner | Refresh |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified demo completion | CRM + analytics dashboard | Growth operations | Weekly |
| Activation definition | Product event specification | Product analytics | On schema change |

## Metric definitions

- **Qualified demo completion:** completed demo form from an eligible company-size segment, excluding test traffic and duplicate submissions.
- **Guide completion:** reader reaches the final actionable section in a consented analytics session; it is a directional engagement signal, not proof of learning.
