# Compliance

## Publish gate

- [ ] The title states the task outcome, not a vague topic.
- [ ] The first paragraph answers the reader's main question.
- [ ] Each metric has a definition, source, owner, and checked date.
- [ ] Customer-identifying data, screenshots, and quotations have written permission.
- [ ] Links are descriptive and the content is usable by keyboard and screen reader.

## Escalation

Comparative, security, pricing, performance, or customer-result claims require approval from the product-marketing lead and legal owner. Record the approver and evidence URL in the publication ticket.
