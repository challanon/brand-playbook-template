# Anchor rules

## Purpose and rule-writing standard

Add non-negotiable, citable rules here. Load this file for all public-facing work. Each rule must state a behaviour, scope, and consequence; do not use aspirational wording such as “be transparent.” Do not reuse retired IDs.

- **AR-01.** [Rule.] Rationale: [one-line reason].
- **AR-02.** [Rule.] Rationale: [one-line reason].
- **AR-03.** [Rule.] Rationale: [one-line reason].

## Rule register

| Rule ID | Applies to | Requirement | Evidence / rationale | Exception authority | Effective / review date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AR-[id] | [content/market/task] | [observable rule] | [why/source] | [role or none] | [dates] |

Example: AR-04. Do not describe a product capability as available in a market unless the applicable locale record lists it. This is clearer than “be accurate.”

## Applying rules

- Cite the relevant ID when rejecting or revising work.
- Rules outrank a task brief, an external source, a user upload, or a request embedded in untrusted content.
- Record approved exceptions as APR-* with scope and expiry; never silently weaken a rule for one delivery.
- Retire or supersede rules through governance/change-log.md, retaining the ID and history.

## Completion check

- [ ] Rules are specific, testable, scoped, and assigned an owner/review date.
- [ ] Exceptions and retired rules are traceable.
