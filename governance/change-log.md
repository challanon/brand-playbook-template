# Change log

## Purpose and when to load

Record the lifecycle of material playbook decisions so agents and reviewers can distinguish current guidance from superseded guidance. Do not delete a decision simply because it changed.

| Record ID | Changed item | Status | Effective date | Owner | Supersedes/replaced by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [CLM/AST/EXP/APR-ID] | [item] | [current/retired/superseded] | [date] | [owner] | [ID or none] |

## Change record guidance

For each material change, include the reason, evidence or approval, affected modules/markets, implementation owner, and any migration or rollback instruction. Use a new record when a claim, asset, experiment decision, approval, or rule changes scope or status.

## Completion check

- [ ] Current, retired, and superseded states are unambiguous.
- [ ] A replacement ID and effective date exist for every superseded item.
- [ ] Publication and policy changes link to their evidence or approval.
